# Loss catalog

| Loss family | Objective and purpose | Main limitations and examples |
|---|---|---|
| Diffusion denoising/noise MSE | Predict injected Gaussian noise or clean x0 at sampled timestep. Training objective, not quality metric. | [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [SSSDnle](notes/Skorik_2024_SSSDnle.md), [MultiODE](notes/Yehuda_2024_MultiODE.md), [PGLRF](notes/Wang_2026_PGLRF.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [MIDT](notes/Huang_2025_MIDT.md), [thesis](notes/Li_2026_EvaluationThesis.md), [GRU-Diffusion](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md), [Neifar](notes/Neifar_2024_DiffECG.md), [SE-Diff](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md), [Qiu](notes/Qiu_2026_PhysDiff.md) |
| VAE ELBO/reconstruction + KL | Expected likelihood/reconstruction and KL posterior-to-prior; balance via beta/schedule. Specify decoder likelihood, reductions and annealing. | [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [cNVAE](notes/Sviridov_2025_cNVAE.md), [FEMVAE](notes/Nishikimi_2024_FEMVAE.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md), [WearECG](notes/Guan_2026_WearECG.md), [Sang](notes/Sang_2025_Personalization.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md) |
| GAN adversarial objectives | Logistic/softplus or Wasserstein critic with gradient penalty; signs and regularizers differ. State exact generator/discriminator losses. | [GAN comparison](notes/Adib_2025_GANComparison.md), [Hu](notes/Hu_2024_DigitalTwin.md), [Zanchi](notes/Zanchi_2026_Validation.md), [Kong](notes/Kong_2024_fGAN.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [MultiODE](notes/Yehuda_2024_MultiODE.md), [Tiwari](notes/Tiwari_2026_Minority.md), [Zhang](notes/Zhang_2026_QualityGAN.md), [CoFE](notes/Jang_2025_CoFE.md), [Adib](notes/Adib_2025_GANComparison.md) |
| Spectral/multiresolution loss | L1/L2 amplitude or log-magnitude difference on FFT/STFT/mel/PSD. Specify windows, overlap, scale and aggregation. | [MIDT](notes/Huang_2025_MIDT.md), [SE-Diff](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md) |
| Physiology/ODE/Euler residual | Penalize derivatives/simulator residual, event timing or cross-lead consistency. Fitted simulator is a prior, not truth; confirm gradients reach generated state. | [MultiODE](notes/Yehuda_2024_MultiODE.md), [PGLRF](notes/Wang_2026_PGLRF.md), [Qiu](notes/Qiu_2026_PhysDiff.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [FEMVAE](notes/Nishikimi_2024_FEMVAE.md) |
| Contrastive/crossmodal alignment | InfoNCE/NT-Xent or cross-entropy for matched PPG-ECG, text-ECG or latent pairs. Report temperature, negatives and patient identity. | [PPGFlowECG](notes/Fang_2025_PPGFlow.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [ECGTwin](notes/Lai_2025_ECGTwin.md) |
| Distribution/correlation complementarity | Mutual information, orthogonality, cross-lead relationships and temporal interference. Estimator and coefficient alter the target. | [Oladunni](notes/Oladunni_2026_Complementarity.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [MIDT](notes/Huang_2025_MIDT.md) |
| Task/classification/semantic objectives | Label CE, diagnostic similarity or text-embedding alignment for conditional generation; keep train objective distinct from downstream evaluator. | [Hu](notes/Hu_2024_DigitalTwin.md), [UniECG](notes/Jin_2025_UniECG.md), [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [ZhangQualityGAN](notes/Zhang_2026_QualityGAN.md), [Tiwari](notes/Tiwari_2026_Minority.md), [Yang](notes/Yang_2024_CECGGAN.md) |
