# Metric catalog

Metric definitions below summarize reviewed implementations. Study links open extraction notes. They are not pooled across incompatible protocols.

| Metric family | Definition and required inputs | Limitations/implementation needs | Studies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paired pointwise MAE/RMSE/MSE | Mean absolute/squared sample error over aligned target and output; lower. Requires true paired signal, stable phase, scale, lead and length. Appropriate for reconstruction/translation, not unconditional random samples. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [PGLRF](notes/Wang_2026_PGLRF.md), [PPGFlowECG](notes/Fang_2025_PPGFlow.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [PhysDiff](notes/Qiu_2026_PhysDiff.md), [WearECG](notes/Guan_2026_WearECG.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md), [FlowECG](notes/Bondar_2025_FlowECG.md) |
| DTW / paired DTW | Minimum accumulated local distance along a monotone temporal warping path; lower. Paired use requires defensible target identity; nearest-sample DTW for unpaired generation is a different statistic and rewards larger sample pools. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [MultiBeat](notes/Pohl_2025_MultiBeat.md), [FlowECG](notes/Bondar_2025_FlowECG.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [FEMVAE](notes/Nishikimi_2024_FEMVAE.md), [thesis privacy DCR](notes/Li_2026_EvaluationThesis.md), [PhysDiff](notes/Qiu_2026_PhysDiff.md) |
| Fréchet distance / FID / FRD | Gaussian 2-Wasserstein distance between feature means/covariances. Extractor, layer, normalization, sample count and training data are part of the metric. Raw-vector FD, ECG-feature FD and FID are not interchangeable. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [MultiBeat](notes/Pohl_2025_MultiBeat.md), [cNVAE](notes/Sviridov_2025_cNVAE.md), [Cardoso](notes/Cardoso_2023_Bayesian.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [GRU-Diffusion](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md), [LA VQ-Editor](notes/Hu_2024_DigitalTwin.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md), [WearECG](notes/Guan_2026_WearECG.md) |
| Manifold precision/recall/coverage/density | kNN/manifold estimates of sample quality and support coverage; precision≈validity, recall≈coverage. Sensitive to k, feature model, reference sample and duplication. Report components and intervals. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md), [Russo thesis](notes/Russo_2025_HumanFeedback.md), [Hu](notes/Hu_2024_DigitalTwin.md) |
| ECG morphology/physiology metrics | Detected HR/RR/HRV, PR/QRS/QT/QTc, ST deviation, P/QRS/T amplitudes/axes, beat counts, rhythm transitions. Define detector, sampling, failures, units, aggregation and condition strata. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [Zanchi](notes/Zanchi_2026_Validation.md), [PGLRF](notes/Wang_2026_PGLRF.md), [thesis](notes/Li_2026_EvaluationThesis.md), [PPGFlowECG](notes/Fang_2025_PPGFlow.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md), [CoFE](notes/Jang_2025_CoFE.md), [MultiODE](notes/Yehuda_2024_MultiODE.md) |
| Spectral/time-frequency metrics | PSD, Fourier/mel distance, wavelet energy, spectral similarity. Define window, normalization, frequency bins and scale. Global spectrum does not localize QRS/ST events. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [MIDT](notes/Huang_2025_MIDT.md), [SE-Diff](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [thesis](notes/Li_2026_EvaluationThesis.md), [CardioPPG](notes/Fang_2025_CardioPPG.md) |
| Classifier utility: TSTR/TRTR | Train on synthetic and test on held-out real, against real-train/real-test baseline with same model, class balance and patient split. Measures task utility, not general clinical validity or fidelity. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [SSSDnle](notes/Skorik_2024_SSSDnle.md), [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [MultiBeat](notes/Pohl_2025_MultiBeat.md), [Hu](notes/Hu_2024_DigitalTwin.md), [MIDT](notes/Huang_2025_MIDT.md), [thesis](notes/Li_2026_EvaluationThesis.md), [GRU-Diffusion](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md), [GAN comparison](notes/Adib_2025_GANComparison.md) |
| Real/fake expert assessment | Blinded randomized balanced sample, expertise, repeated rating, agreement and confidence required. Chance-level detection is perceptual indistinguishability under that protocol, not safety or diagnostic equivalence. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [SSSDnle](notes/Skorik_2024_SSSDnle.md), [DiffuSETS](notes/Lai_2025_DiffuSETS.md), [Zanchi2025](notes/Zanchi_2025_Brugada.md), [Zanchi2026](notes/Zanchi_2026_Validation.md), [PPGFlowECG](notes/Fang_2025_PPGFlow.md), [WearECG](notes/Guan_2026_WearECG.md), [Russo thesis](notes/Russo_2025_HumanFeedback.md) |
| Privacy/MIA | Explicit threat-model attacks on membership, nearest-neighbor/memorization, linkage and sensitive attributes; report AUC and low-FPR TPR with matched member/nonmember cohorts. One heuristic is not a guarantee. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [MIDT](notes/Huang_2025_MIDT.md), [Hu](notes/Hu_2024_DigitalTwin.md), [thesis](notes/Li_2026_EvaluationThesis.md), [SSSDnle discussion](notes/Skorik_2024_SSSDnle.md) |
| Efficiency | Measure sampling steps and wall-clock/energy/memory on named hardware, batch, output length and quality target. FLOPs/parameter count alone do not establish deployed latency. | Use within-study; retain pairedness, extractor, split and sample-count details. | [FlowECG](notes/Bondar_2025_FlowECG.md), [CLEPGAN](notes/Li_2025_CLEPGAN.md), [P2Es](notes/Ji_2026_P2Es.md), [GRU-Diffusion](notes/Wang_2025_SEDiff.md) |
