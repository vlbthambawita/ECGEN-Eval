# P2Es: Physiology-Conditioned Diffusion for 12-Lead ECG Generation from PPG

**Citation:** Ji H and Gao W and Zhou P. *P2Es: Physiology-Conditioned Diffusion for 12-Lead ECG Generation from PPG*. Smart health (Amsterdam, Netherlands), 2026. DOI: 10.1016/j.smhl.2026.100684. arXiv: 2509.25480v1; first public: 2025-09-29; peer-reviewed date: 2026-07-13.

**Source/version:** [paper.pdf](../papers/2026/Ji_2026_P2Es/paper.pdf); [landing.html](../papers/2026/Ji_2026_P2Es/landing.html). [Landing page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.smhl.2026.100684).


**Task and model:** Conditional diffusion translates PPG to 12-lead ECG. A domain-adjacent waveform synthesis study included because it reports generated waveform evaluation (pp.5–8).

**Data and split:** MIMIC II/III/IV after overlap removal; 125 Hz, 2-s windows, subject-wise z-score, patient-level 80/20 split; final merged set count not stated in inspected methods. Ablations and bidirectional III↔IV cross-dataset assessment (pp.7,11–12).

**Evaluation metrics:** Paired-reference MSE, multilead DTW, variance of waveform error, KL between selected feature distributions, marginal Wasserstein, and limb-lead electrical-axis cosine similarity (Table 1 p.8). This is translation fidelity. MACs/latency/model size separate; no expert or privacy assessment.

**Training objectives:** Contrastive PPG-to-affinity-group cross entropy Eq.6 (temperature unspecified). Total Eq.18 = lambda1 interlead relation error + lambda2 Fourier-domain L2 + lambda3 axis L2 + lambda4 alignment penalty; coefficients 1,.5,.2,.3. Alignment Eq.17 sums R-peak position, amplitude-range, and heart-rate-count discrepancy. Bespoke frequency-blur/QRS-adaptive corruption is process design, not a separately identified additive training loss (pp.5–7).

**Reported evidence:** MIMIC IV full model Table2 MSE .0902, DTW .0583, error variance .4647, KL .4920, MW 8.0902, axis cosine .9542. Ablations remove affinity, frequency processing, multiscale reverse architecture; full best. 50 DDIM steps, reported 81% faster than 1000-step DDPM (pp.7–12).

**Clinical utility, experts and privacy:** No clear TSTR with independent classifier trained only on generated ECG. Patient-level split and cross-dataset reverse-direction degradation MIMICIII→IV MSE .1056 vs within .0914 (p.12). No clinician/privacy protocol.

**Reproducibility:** Substantial implementation and split detail, 57M+ parameters; code/checkpoint availability not verified. Exact final sample count and some loss scale details unavailable.

**Critical assessment:** Paired errors measure translation with paired targets and aligned windows. Multiple baselines share protocol, but clinical-grade claims exceed the outcomes; authors note single-lead ECG cannot substitute for diagnostic 12-lead ECG.
