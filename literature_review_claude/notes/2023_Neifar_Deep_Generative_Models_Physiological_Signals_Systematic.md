# Deep Generative Models for Physiological Signals: A Systematic Literature Review
- **ID / Year / Venue:** P170 / 2023 / arXiv (Cornell University)
- **Model & task:** Systematic review of deep generative models for physiological signals (ECG, EEG, PPG, EMG)
- **Losses:** Catalogues GAN BCE, WGAN(-GP), LSGAN, SeqGAN policy gradient, VAE recon+KL, diffusion objectives
- **Evaluation:** Task-organised metric catalogue (RMSE, MAE, MMD, PRD, DTW, FID most common; CRPS, TWED, Chamfer, SSIM etc.)
- **Data:** 71 studies; MIT-BIH, PTB-XL, BIDMC most frequent ECG sets
- **Takeaway for ECGEN-Eval:** Metric catalogue confirms ECGEN-Eval's core set; consider CRPS for imputation and TWED/Soft-DTW as alternatives.
