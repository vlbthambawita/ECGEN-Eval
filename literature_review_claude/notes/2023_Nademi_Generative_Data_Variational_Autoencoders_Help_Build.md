# Generative Data by β-Variational Autoencoders Help Build Stronger Classifiers: ECG Use Case
- **ID / Year / Venue:** P086 / 2023 / International Symposium on Medical Information Processing and Analysis (SIPAIM)
- **Model & task:** VAE (beta-VAE, beta=8, 32-d latent, van de Leur architecture on 12-lead rhythm); augmentation of CPSC 2018 with STE ECGs generated by posterior sampling from real AB STE ECGs
- **Losses:** beta-VAE (reconstruction + beta-weighted KL)
- **Evaluation:** InceptionTime per-label F1 on CPSC 2018 with bootstrap 95% CIs vs real-data and oversampling controls; XGBoost AUROC/F1 of embeddings; embedding correlation with Philips measurements [utility-TSTR]
- **Data:** private Alberta 12-lead (244,077 patients); CPSC 2018; leads: 12; fs: 500 Hz; length: 10 s (4096 samples)
- **Notes:** STE F1 +0.046 with 1072 VAE ECGs vs +0.089 with real STE ECGs and +0.045 with oversampling; 4 other labels improved but STD F1 fell -0.13. No signal-level fidelity, clinical-feature or privacy evaluation; authors themselves flag MSE as inadequate and propose P-wave duration etc. as future metrics.
- **Takeaway for ECGEN-Eval:** Utility-only papers need fidelity and memorisation checks: encoder-posterior 'generation' yields near-reconstructions, so ECGEN-Eval's nn_dist and clinical-interval comparisons should accompany any augmentation-gain claim.
