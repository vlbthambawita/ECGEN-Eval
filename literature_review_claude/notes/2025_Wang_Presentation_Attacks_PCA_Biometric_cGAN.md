# Presentation Attacks on the PCA-Based Biometric System Using cGAN-Generated ECGs
- **ID / Year / Venue:** P183 / 2025 / Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBC)
- **Model & task:** GAN (conditional GAN, pix2pix-style: FC + 4 transposed-conv generator, FC + 4 conv discriminator); other (beat + RR-interval conditioned continuous ECG trace synthesis for presentation attacks on ECG biometrics)
- **Losses:** standard conditional GAN adversarial loss (generator and discriminator updated alternately)
- **Evaluation:** FPIR_attack and FPIR increase over zero-effort attack (at EER threshold) against a PCA-based biometric identification system; FNIR/FPIR/EER of the biometric baseline; inter-beat correlation within synthetic vs real traces; R-R interval variability (qualitative); visual inspection of Q-wave morphology; sensitivity to number of training subjects and pairs [fidelity, privacy, diversity]
- **Data:** PTB Diagnostic ECG (lead I; 285 usable subjects: 100 registered, 85 intruders); leads: 1 (lead I); fs: 125 (downsampled from 1000) Hz; length: 3.2 (four-beat trace, 400 samples; conditioning beat 0.8 s) s
- **Notes:** Synthetic traces built from template-reconstructed heartbeats raise FPIR by 5.75 percentage points over the 5.39% EER zero-effort baseline (ns=85, np=180). Inter-beat correlation of synthetic traces 0.934 ± 0.122 vs 0.989 ± 0.016 for real (excess beat-to-beat variability), RR variability lower than real and Q waves shallower; RR intervals are random draws within a normal range, so rhythm realism is not modelled. Relevant as a re-identification/privacy-attack use of synthetic ECGs rather than a quality benchmark.
- **Takeaway for ECGEN-Eval:** Demonstrates that subject-identifying morphology survives in conditional synthetic ECGs; ECGEN-Eval's nn_dist/privacy section should add a re-identification test (an ECG biometric matcher's identification rate on synthetic vs real) and within-record inter-beat consistency as a realism check.
